How it works
Two ways to grow
Fundukuzama gives your SME two tracks — grow your own capital automatically, and raise more from investors when you're ready to scale.
📈 Grow

Connect your sales channel and Fundukuzama automatically invests a set % of every sale into a diversified portfolio — money market, SME shares, and reinvestment. Track it all from My Investments.

🏙 Raise

Once you've built a track record, list your business on the Raise Capital marketplace. Investors fund you via revenue-share — no equity given up, repaid as a % of future sales.
