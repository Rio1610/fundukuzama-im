📋 What is a revenue-share agreement?

Instead of giving away ownership of your business (equity), you agree to pay back a percentage of your monthly revenue to investors until a set return is reached. You keep 100% of your business.

Example: You raise R50,000. You agree to pay 8% of monthly revenue until investors receive R65,000 total. Once paid, the agreement ends — you owe nothing more.
