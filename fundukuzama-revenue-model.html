Launch sequence — from idea to live platform
1
Register a (Pty) Ltd
CIPC online — R175, 1–5 business days. Opens your business bank account and makes you a legal entity.
2
Partner with Franc + Stitch Money
They hold the FSP licenses. You integrate their APIs to split payments at checkout and auto-invest the 30% — no FSCA application needed yet.
3
Build and launch the combined platform
SME sales-linking + investment dashboard. The site design and logo are already done — wire to real APIs and go live.
4
List 5–10 SMEs via revenue-share agreements
Not equity — a revenue-share contract. A business raises capital and pays back a % of monthly revenue. Keeps you outside securities law while you grow.
5
Apply for Category I FSP once you have traction
R2,697 application fee. 2–6 month process. By then you have real user numbers, real returns, and a story the FSCA and future investors want to hear.
