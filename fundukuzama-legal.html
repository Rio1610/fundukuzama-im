Terms of Service

By creating a Fundukuzama account, you agree to use the platform to auto-invest a portion of your business's sales revenue and, where applicable, to raise or provide growth capital to other SMEs via revenue-share arrangements.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the business information you submit, for maintaining the security of your account, and for complying with South African tax and financial regulations applicable to your business.

Privacy Policy

Fundukuzama collects the business and personal information you provide at signup (business name, contact details, sales data connected via your payment channel) to operate your account, process auto-investments, and facilitate revenue-share funding between SMEs.

We do not sell your data. Information is shared only with the regulated financial partners needed to process investments and payments on your behalf (e.g. payment and investment API providers).

Revenue-Sharing Agreement

Auto-invested funds are not a loan and carry investment risk — value can go up or down. Funds raised by an SME through the marketplace are provided by funding SMEs in exchange for a fixed percentage of the receiving SME's future sales, until an agreed repayment cap is reached. This is not an equity or debt instrument; no ownership stake or fixed-interest obligation is created.

Marketplace Terms

Businesses listing on the Fundukuzama marketplace to raise capital consent to a business review process, including verification of sales history and business registration, before being listed for funding. Fundukuzama may decline or remove listings that do not meet its review criteria.
